Honest Hours privacy policy

Honest Hours (Honest Work Hours Tracker, com.apprizon.hours) is published by Apprizon LLC.

Honest Hours stores the jobs, shifts, breaks, rates and notes you enter on your phone. It has no accounts and no server of ours, and we do not receive what you enter.

If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. You control that in Android's settings. Backup files and timesheets you save or share go only where you send them.

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play.

The app shows no ads and does not use the advertising ID.

To delete your data, delete the shifts or jobs in the app, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete.
