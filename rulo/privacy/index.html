Rulo privacy policy

Rulo (Rulo: Tape Measure & AR Ruler, com.apprizon.measure) is a measuring app for Android published by Apprizon LLC.

We collect nothing. Rulo has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We receive no information about you or how you use the app.

The camera. Rulo uses the camera to measure. The camera picture is processed on your phone to find surfaces and is not stored or sent anywhere. A picture is kept only when you choose to save one: to your phone's gallery, or to Rulo's saved list.

Google Play Services for AR. This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What stays on your phone. Your measurements, the pictures you save to the list, their names and notes, and your settings are stored on your phone only. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may copy them to your own Google account along with the rest of your phone; you control that in Android's settings. Files you export or share go only where you send them.

Purchases. If you buy the unlock, the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play.

Permissions. Rulo asks for the camera only. Internet access is used only by Google Play's billing library for the purchase. The app does not use the advertising ID.

Deleting your data. Delete measurements in the app, uninstall it, or clear its storage in Android's settings. Pictures saved to the gallery stay there until you delete them. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children. Rulo is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
