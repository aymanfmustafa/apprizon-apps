Shortlist privacy policy

Shortlist (Shortlist: Minimalist Launcher, com.apprizon.shortlist) is a home-screen app for Android published by Apprizon LLC.

We collect nothing. Shortlist has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. We receive no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your phone. To show your apps, Shortlist reads the list of apps that have a home-screen entry. That list, your home screen, the names you give apps, the apps you hide or pause, and your settings are stored on your phone only. If Android's backup is turned on, Android may copy Shortlist's settings to your own Google account along with the rest of your phone; you control that in Android's settings. If you save your settings to a file, the file goes only where you put it.

Purchases. If you buy Focus tools, the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play.

Permissions. Shortlist can open the notification shade when you swipe down, and can list the apps in Android's private space while it is unlocked. Internet access is used only by Google Play's billing library for the purchase. The app does not use the advertising ID.

Deleting your data. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android's settings. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children. Shortlist is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
